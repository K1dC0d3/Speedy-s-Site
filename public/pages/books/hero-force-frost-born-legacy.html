Volume 3, "Frost Born Legacy," plunges the Hero Force into a frozen nightmare when a mysterious new ally arrives with a devastating warning. Calling himself Glaze, this ice-wielding newcomer claims to be an outcast seeking refuge.

As he integrates into the group and forms deep bonds with the team, a bitter secret threatens to shatter their newfound trust from the inside out.

The stakes reach a freezing point when a colossal airborne fortress appears, encasing the city in unbreakable ice and unleashing a cold, ruthless army upon Earth.

Caught off guard by a calculating villain with an ancient agenda, the Hero Force finds their powers compromised and their team dynamic pushed to its absolute breaking point.

To save their frozen home from total destruction, Earth’s defenders must navigate deep betrayals, forge unlikely alliances, and unite their ultimate powers in a high-octane battle against an icy threat that endangers the entire world.
