Hero Force:
The Origin

Powered by cosmic fate, five ordinary teens rise up together to defend Earth

✦

Volume 1, "The Origin," opens with a catastrophic event across the galaxy: the destruction of the planet Urtah. When the ruthless warlord Zorak and his invading Kruxlien army attempt to seize the planet’s ultimate life source—the Urtah Star—a desperate struggle triggers a massive explosion, sending a powerful, energy-infused fragment hurtling across the cosmos directly toward Earth.

In the small, unsuspecting town of Hansville, several teenagers are living entirely separate lives, unaware that their paths are about to collide. Lloyd is a superhero enthusiast following old journals in search of local legends alongside his loyal friends, Anna and Alex.

Meanwhile, thrill-seekers Jay and Korra are looking for their next high-stakes adventure, and Vallery, an artistic outcast, seeks refuge from her quiet struggles in the solace of the forest.

Everything changes in a single shocking moment when the alien fragment violently strikes Hansville. A blinding surge of cosmic energy sweeps across the area, forever altering the teenagers and embedding raw, extraordinary abilities within them. But their life-changing transformation does not go unnoticed.

Sensing the active star power, Zorak’s Kruxlien forces descend upon Earth in full force, determined to hunt down the teens and extract the cosmic energy residing within them by any means necessary.

As an overwhelming alien armada looms overhead and threatens to tear their world apart, the group is thrust into an epic struggle for survival. Divided by fear, tested by internal tension, and facing an impossible extraterrestrial threat, these reluctant allies must overcome their deepest doubts and personal limits.

To defend their hometown, protect the people they love, and stop an all-out planetary invasion, they will have to unite, unlock their true potential, and discover what it genuinely means to step up as Earth's final line of defense.
