The Imagination

A world where imagination becomes reality

✦

The Imagination Book 1 follows a group of secondary school students at OBMS who discover a portal to the Imaginarium, a metaphysical dimension powered by human creativity.

Led by Joshua (Jayden), the friends are recruited by a central intelligence named Cerebella to serve as Guardians against The Reality, a faction seeking to delete imagination and enforce rigid order.

Transformed into animated versions of themselves with specialized tools, they successfully navigate a psychological loop within a teenager's mind to resolve her grief. Returning to their daily school lives, they must balance academic pressures, with their secret responsibilities.

The narrative concludes with the team being summoned for a new mission involving a famous young creator whose creativity is failing under the weight of online criticism.
