The Imagination

The story follows a group of secondary school students at OBMS—Joshua, David, Ifeoma, Eva, Setemi, and Doyin — who discover a hidden world through a portal in their school's back wall.

After Joshua triggers the portal by saying the word "creativity," the group is transported to the Imaginarium, a collective manifestation of human imagination.

There, they meet Cerebella, the central intelligence of ImagiCore, who reveals that they have been chosen as Guardians because their minds are uniquely compatible with imaginative construction. As Guardians, the teenagers undergo training to use a system that allows them to manifest tools and weapons from their thoughts.

They are assigned operational codenames to protect their identities: Jayden (Joshua), Kara (Ifeoma), Iris (Eva), Viper (Setemi), Nikki (Doyin), and Skylar (David)

Their primary mission is to protect the Imaginarium from The Reality, a mysterious group that views imagination as a flaw and seeks to delete it to create a fixed, controlled reality

The plot is centered around two major missions:

Mission 1: The Loop (Zoe): The Guardians travel via the Mind Glider to the mindscape of a girl named Zoe, which is stuck in a repetitive loop due to her grief over her father's death.
They battle monsters representing her anxiety, anger, and depression.

Eventually, they reach Zoe’s consciousness and help her stabilize her world by encouraging her to take "one step" at a time to process her grief.

Mission 2: The City of Ideas (Pizzy B): They are tasked with saving the mindscape of Pizzy B, a world-famous young creator whose "City of Ideas" is collapsing due to his fear of failure following online criticism.

To combat this "creative collapse," the group activates Hero Mode, which grants them unique superpowers: Jayden (electricity), Kara (artistic construction), Skylar (super speed), Iris (wind), Viper (primal enhancement/strength), and Nikki (spectral phasing/flight)

The climax occurs when The Reality agents arrive at Pizzy B’s mindscape to delete it.
These agents use a devastating ability called "Sever" to disconnect the Guardians from their powers.
Despite a desperate attempt by Jayden to stop them, the mission fails; the Reality agents successfully delete Pizzy B’s imagination, leaving his real-world body in a coma

The book ends with a major twist: back at school, two classmates, Simi and Michael, accidentally witness the Guardians emerging from the portal

Furthermore, it is revealed that The Reality agents are actually four other girls from their school — Moyo, Jomiloju, Ogo, and Yonu—who are working for a masked figure at a company called Aurelion Technologies Their school principal is also revealed to be an accomplice, helping to hide their absences during missions
